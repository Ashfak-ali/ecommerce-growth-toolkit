A high-performance, open-source mathematical decision engine designed for e-commerce operators, D2C founders, media buyers, and supply chain inventory planners.

Extracted from real-world high-volume storefronts handling **20,000+ active SKUs** and powered by the mathematical models developed at [ka36.in](https://ka36.in/).

---

## ⚡ Included Calculators & Engines

1. **Meta & Google Ads ROAS Forecaster**: Forecasts projected monthly gross revenue and net profit based on advertising spend and ROAS target multiples.
2. **Break-Even ROAS Target**: Computes the minimum ROAS threshold required to guarantee profitable unit economics after COGS, shipping, and fulfillment fees.
3. **CRO Revenue Uplift Engine**: Projects monthly and annual top-line revenue uplift from percentage-point conversion rate gains against baseline traffic and AOV.
4. **Inventory Reorder Point (ROP)**: Predicts the exact warehouse inventory stock level at which a new purchase order must be issued, incorporating supplier lead-time demand and safety buffer stock.
5. **Standardized SKU String Generator**: Generates normalized, compliant catalog identifier codes for Amazon, Flipkart, Blinkit, and Shopify multi-channel warehousing.
6. **Customer Lifetime Value (CLV/LTV)**: Evaluates projected cumulative gross revenue per customer across retention cycles and annual order frequencies.

## 🧮 Mathematical Reference Formulas

### 1. Break-Even ROAS Formula
$$\text{Gross Margin} = \text{Selling Price} - \text{COGS}$$
$$\text{Break-Even ROAS} = \frac{\text{Selling Price}}{\text{Gross Margin}}$$

### 2. Inventory Reorder Point (ROP) Formula
$$\text{ROP} = (\text{Daily Demand} \times \text{Lead Time}) + (\text{Daily Demand} \times \text{Safety Buffer Days})$$

### 3. Customer Lifetime Value (LTV) Formula
$$\text{LTV} = \text{AOV} \times \text{Purchase Frequency/Year} \times \text{Customer Lifespan (Years)}$$
